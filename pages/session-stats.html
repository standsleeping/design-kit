Session stats footer scroll, click, move, switch tabs

What this is

The strip pinned at the bottom is the SessionStatsFooter component. It tracks six live values: time the tab has been on (visible and focused), time it has been off (hidden or in a background window), the maximum scroll percentage reached in this content region (capped at 100), the number of tab switches away from this tab, the total mouse movements, and the total clicks anywhere in the window. Durations are shown as m:ss.

Move your mouse to watch moves climb. Click anywhere to bump clicks. Switch to another browser tab and back to bump tabs. Scroll this region to push scroll % up; it only ratchets, never decreases.
