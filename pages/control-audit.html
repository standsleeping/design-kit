Design Kit

Control Audit

Surfaces inconsistencies in how buttons, inputs, and interactive chrome are sized, padded, and bordered across the kit. Each control is rendered once and measured at first paint, so the diagnostic table reflects the live computed values, not the source declarations. The kit recognizes two tiers: a control tier (buttons + inputs) that must hold square padding and peer-aligned dimensions, and a chrome tier with a narrow, named roster whose asymmetric padding is structural by design (see visual-language.md § "Chrome with structural asymmetry"). Chrome rows still report their measurements but are excluded from the square-padding pass/fail tally.

Diagnostics

Per-control computed values, measured live. The Square? column flags any control whose block-axis padding differs from its inline-axis padding (a violation of SQUARE_PADDING_DEFAULT). The Floor column reports the height floor: explicit (min-height set), or implicit (content-driven, grows with font-size). The Focus column reports the focus pattern declared in CSS.

Cross-component pairs

The framework split: when a button and an input sit next to each other, do they read as peers? Each card renders the pair and reports the delta. Large deltas mean side-by-side composition will look misaligned.

What this audit does not catch

This page measures static rendered values. It does not capture: focus-ring clipping under scroll containers (see INSET_FOCUS_RING); hover/active state divergence; touch-target enforcement under coarse pointers; text-baseline drift (see VERTICAL_METRIC_DRIFT). Those need their own probes. For now, eyeball the gallery above in both light and dark themes; this page declares color-scheme: light dark and respects the OS preference.
