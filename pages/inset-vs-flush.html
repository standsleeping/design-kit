Inset vs Flush
Mode A · Surfaces

Inset

Bounded objects with four borders, square corners, square internal padding. Each owns its boundary and floats in its parent's gutter.

Same rows · inset cards

Each row carries its own four-side border. Siblings are separated by gutter, not by a shared rule.

Mode B · Items

Flush

Rows inside a surface or pane. Full-bleed background, no four-side border, separated from siblings by a single shared rule.

Same rows · flush items

Rows extend pane edge-to-edge. The active row carries a left border (medium, color-link) and a full-bleed selected-bg fill.
